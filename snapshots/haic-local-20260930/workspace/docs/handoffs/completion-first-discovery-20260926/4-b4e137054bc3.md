## fact

CEM scores ensemble uncertainty and the package wrapper already has a policy action available when planning returns no result, times out, or yields an invalid plan. `PlanResult` does not currently expose selected-sequence uncertainty.

## inference

A valid planner-selected action in a model-uncertain region may be less reliable than the actor fallback. Returning to the already computed actor action under a predeclared uncertainty condition may avoid some model-driven failures. This is untested.
