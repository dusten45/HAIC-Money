# Agent handoff template

Assignment, owner, scope, hypothesis ID, and plan revision/hash:

## fact

Observed facts only, with counts and conditions.

## inference

Explanations supported by the facts; label untested mechanisms.

## unknown

Missing evidence, unresolved conflicts, and validity limits.

## recommendation

Next action and its approval gate, control, endpoint, and falsifier.

## source_paths

Paths or URLs supporting each fact and inference. Do not copy legacy raw data into the handoff.
