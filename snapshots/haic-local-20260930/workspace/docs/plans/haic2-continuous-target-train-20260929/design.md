# TRAIN-only continuous target speed imitation

This is direction 3 in the four-mechanism post-held-out batch. The prior bounded gas student passed TRAIN and TUNE but lost one valid held-out finish, so it is rejected. This cycle learns a different representation: a continuous target speed from a TRAIN-only geometry teacher rather than a boost/hold/coast class. The teacher watches unchanged stable pixel-control rollouts in shadow mode; its actions are never executed for collection. Successful trajectories alone supply labels. The held-out 5228-5231 identities and any other non-TRAIN identities supply no labels or threshold choices.

Collect tracks 1-3 x previously unused TRAIN seeds 5032-5035, twelve control episodes, maximum 2,000 decisions each. Seeds 5032-5034 per track train a 21-feature pixel regressor; seed 5035 per track is within-TRAIN validation. Target is the shadow teacher's continuous upcoming-curve speed in [28,60]. Use fixed 48-48 ReLU architecture, Adam 0.003 for 75 epochs, smooth-L1 loss with predeclared additional weight on low-speed targets. Save a tensor-only weights-only-readable checkpoint and distribution/MAE metrics. At inference the regressor sees pixels and inherited pixel action only; no geometry or simulator state is loaded.

Activation gate: at least 100 teacher targets below 55 in successful training rows and 20 such targets in successful validation rows; at least 20 validation predictions below 55; finite validation loss; validation target MAE at least 10% lower than the constant-60 baseline; safe checkpoint round trip; teacher actions not executed. If this fails, reject without changing architecture, weights or cutoff on these cells. If it passes, separately register fresh TRAIN driving comparison. This run is training activation, not candidate completion or official score. Linux CPU timeout 1,800 seconds, no external action.
