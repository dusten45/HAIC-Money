# TRAIN-only pixel hold, boost and coast shadow distillation

The previous pixel student used binary geometry-teacher labels and learned mostly boost (1,394 of 1,567 eligible successful-trajectory examples). Its fixed gas 0.28 policy gained lap time but lost valid completion in exact ZIP confirmation. Train a new student on three teacher actions: hold the inherited pixel driver's gas, raise it to 0.28, or coast at zero gas. The teacher may use simulator geometry only while labeling states visited by the unchanged stable pixel driver on TRAIN episodes. Its suggestions are never executed during collection. Runtime uses only pixels and inherited pixel-driver actions.

Register official tracks 1–3 × unused TRAIN seeds 5016–5019. Use only validly completed stable-driver trajectories; 5016–5018 are model training rows and 5019 is within-TRAIN validation. Apply the existing 21 pixel feature contract and clear-road/low-steer eligibility to all three labels. Fit one fixed 21→48→3 classifier for 50 epochs with inverse-square-root class weighting derived only from TRAIN counts, capped at 0.5–4.0. Runtime accepts a boost or coast class only when its probability is at least 0.65; otherwise the base pixel action persists. Save normalization and weights as tensor-only checkpoint. No raw frames or geometry labels are copied into the package.

The 12-rollout run is capped at 2,000 decisions per episode, Linux CPU, 1,800 seconds. Record valid control completion, class counts by split, finite loss, validation prediction counts, coast precision/recall, checkpoint SHA-256, duration and resource metrics. Advance to a separately registered TRAIN closed-loop driving diagnostic only if completed controls provide at least 50 coast training labels and 10 coast validation labels; losses are finite; at least 10 accepted validation coast predictions have precision at least 0.60 and recall at least 0.20; and checkpoint loading remains pixel-only. Otherwise reject/revise without tuning class thresholds on validation. No TUNE, held-out, confirmation, blind, official or external action is included. A training result is not a candidate completion score.
