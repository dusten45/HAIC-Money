The current strongest verified local package in this workspace is the frozen visual safe/fast mode-switch ZIP at `tmp/hazard-flow-batch-20260929/artifacts/haic-research-v2/hazard-mode-switch-exact-package-20260929/submission.zip`, SHA-256 `b0dc7a24e6ef994cee6e663ec6605f5aa088dbb9b3ce4510f90ccd16dc9d7fc4`. Its registered extracted-ZIP confirmation on tracks 1-3 x seeds 3008-3011 finished 12/12 versus the prior selected exact ZIP 12/12, with median finished lap 24.07 versus 25.96 seconds and all twelve paired laps faster. Peak RSS was 302.1 MB, maximum import+create 1.78 seconds, maximum act 83.3 ms, and invalid actions zero. This is local evidence only: current official-site compliance and private-track performance remain unknown; no site action is authorized.

A subsequent four-direction hand-designed speed batch in `tmp/mode-speed-batch-20260929/` found first-clear entry, safe-steering pedal fusion and road-width occupancy behaviorally duplicate on TRAIN and each lost a control finish. Curve-exit surge activated, then finished 11/12 versus unchanged mode switch 12/12 on fresh TUNE 3040-3043. Those cells are consumed. Our separate bounded-gas, continuous-target and pixel-bend efforts also did not pass held-out or activation gates. The next batch uses the exact mode-switch ZIP as frozen control and searches mechanisms that learn temporal risk or action value from TRAIN-only experience rather than adjusting the same clear-road threshold.

At most four candidates will enter the initial batch, one per independent direction. Every executable cycle needs its own exact v2 plan hash, source copy, separate standing-authority design/execution records, split identities, denominator, falsifier and resource limit. No threshold or weight sweep occurs before mechanism activation. Candidate and control run on matched newly assigned cells; completion ranks first, then median finished lap and the registered tie-breakers. TRAIN failures may support diagnosis, while TUNE, held-out, confirmation and blind identities stay disjoint and never become training labels.

1. **Temporal pixel risk predictor.** TRAIN-only paired trajectories label whether a recent pixel/action history precedes loss of road visibility, collision or qualified finish failure. A pixel-only recurrent or fixed-window predictor grants the safe policy full authority when risk rises. Activation requires calibrated warning lead time and an actual change in fast/safe decisions; falsify on late or widespread false alarms. Control: exact mode-switch source/ZIP, never inferred map identity.
2. **Action-conditioned visual forward model.** On TRAIN rollouts, fit a pixel feature transition model that predicts near/far road-center and visibility changes under the safe and fast actions. At runtime choose the fast action only when predicted visible-road margin remains adequate for a short horizon. Falsify if counterfactual predictions cannot beat a constant-motion baseline or the chooser collapses to one mode. Use only pixels and actions at inference; simulator geometry is evaluation-only.
3. **Constrained policy distillation.** Imitate the exact mode-switch actions on successful TRAIN trajectories, then optimize a compact pixel policy for lower lap time under a preregistered completion constraint and unchanged fallback. Falsify if it does not change throttle/steering materially or loses valid completion in fresh matched cells. Distilled teacher actions come from the frozen local ZIP, not private tracks.
4. **Episode-level visual risk routing.** Train a pixel-only early-history selector from matched TRAIN outcomes of the frozen safe and fast actors, then commit to an actor for an episode. Falsify if held-back TRAIN validation cannot identify failure-prone episodes or route decisions collapse to one actor. No track ID, seed ID, simulator position or leaderboard information enters runtime.

The smallest next experiment is a registered TRAIN-only feasibility study for direction 1: collect paired trajectories on new TRAIN cells, freeze a validation subset, and measure whether pixel-history warning lead time and false alarms support safe intervention before any candidate driving. If not, reject that direction and move to the next. Preserve the exact mode-switch ZIP and prior outcomes unchanged. Official upload, submission and model confirmation remain outside this research batch.
