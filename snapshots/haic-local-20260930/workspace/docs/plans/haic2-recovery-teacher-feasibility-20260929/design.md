# TRAIN-only privileged recovery teacher feasibility

This tests whether a recoverable steering action exists before training a pixel student. Frozen control is exact visual mode-switch ZIP SHA-256 `b0dc7a24e6ef994cee6e663ec6605f5aa088dbb9b3ce4510f90ccd16dc9d7fc4` and behaviorally identical source. Both arms execute that controller until fewer than three pixel-road center rows remain. The teacher then reads the TRAIN simulator track and car pose, finds the closest centerline point and a centerline goal 12 meters forward, and substitutes its clipped +/-0.7 heading-error steer. Gas and brake remain inherited. The teacher reads privileged state only in this TRAIN diagnostic and cannot be a submission candidate. No map/seed or simulator state enters the control arm.

Six consumed TRAIN cells per arm: 1:5052, 2:5052, 2:5053, 2:5054, 3:5052, 3:5055. Control has six known finishes, three long visibility-loss intervals. Require all six teacher finishes, at least 20 changed steering actions, zero invalid actions, and >=10-decision reduction in longest low-visibility interval on at least two of the three slow-loss cells. If this fails, reject the teacher before any student training. If it passes, next register fresh TRAIN feasibility and a separate pixel distillation study; teacher laps never enter submission-candidate completion rates. Record full trajectories, action latency, collision/damage, reset/import and resource use. Linux CPU, 2 cores, 2 GiB, 1,200 seconds, 2,000 decisions/episode. No external action.
