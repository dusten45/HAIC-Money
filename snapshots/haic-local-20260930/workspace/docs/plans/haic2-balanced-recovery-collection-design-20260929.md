# Balanced TRAIN recovery-state generation

## Purpose and evidence

The pixel-only student should learn when and how to apply the TRAIN privileged recovery teacher without access to track coordinates at inference. The fresh teacher feasibility run preserved all valid control finishes and shortened eight long visibility-loss intervals, but its 110 labels came from only eight episodes and were 108 left / two right. The stronger exact bend-rebound ZIP is the current local control (SHA-256 `8f7e87c29e92caf49db0070842dbc30f21a20ff98a3f48bf17d9599490559e49`). An attempted passive pixel collection ended through Docker infrastructure failure; its 11 partial files are quarantined. Ten of those 11 files contained only left-steer labels, and only one had three right-steer labels. More passive seeds alone are therefore an inefficient way to obtain independent right recovery examples.

## Fixed mechanism

Use the unchanged bend-rebound pixel driver to enter a normal TRAIN episode. Once a full seven-row road is visible, the near road is centered, no pixel obstacle is detected, and the first 80 decisions have elapsed, inject one fixed eight-decision steering pulse. One arm steers left (`-0.7`), the other right (`+0.7`), keeping the driver's gas and brake. After the pulse, the unchanged privileged teacher takes over only when fewer than three pixel-road center rows are visible. Save the actual four-frame pixel stack, teacher steer, inherited driver action, pulse arm, and decision index at every teacher decision. The pulse is TRAIN-only state generation and never enters the student or submission runtime. Do not use simulator road geometry to decide when to pulse; it remains restricted to the TRAIN teacher label.

Start with an activation diagnostic on fresh TRAIN tracks 1–3 × seeds 5140–5141, both pulse arms per cell (12 episodes). Control is the same unpulsed bend-rebound driver on those six cells, observed only for state and finish context. The diagnostic is not a submission-candidate completion comparison. Record per-arm pulse start and completed pulse, whether road visibility actually dropped below three rows, teacher label counts and signs, valid actions, finish, collision/damage and latency. Exact pulse magnitude and duration are fixed before this run; do not sweep them on these cells.

## Decision and next stage

Activation advances only if both arms execute the full pulse on at least four of six distinct cells, at least three distinct left-pulse cells yield positive teacher steering in real, unmirrored simulator states, at least three distinct right-pulse cells yield negative teacher steering, and all observations/actions/labels are finite with zero invalid actions. A failure revises the state-generation mechanism on a new TRAIN block; it is not a score regression. If activation passes, register a separate larger TRAIN collection with whole-cell partitions and at least 20 recovery cells / five right-recovery cells before training a pixel student. Report induced and unperturbed episodes separately; do not count mirrored samples as independent right episodes. Student driving must later beat the exact bend-rebound ZIP on preregistered fresh cells, using completion before lap time. Keep TUNE, held-out, confirmation and blind cells out of fitting. No official-site action is included.

Linux CPU limit: two cores, 2 GiB, 1,800 seconds, at most 2,000 decisions per episode. Register the exact source hashes, control ZIP, split, six map/seed identities, 12-arm denominator, falsifier and approval events under a new v2 plan before running.
