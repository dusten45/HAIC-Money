# Next completion-first pixel-speed research batch

The binary pixel student learned mostly positive shadow labels (1,394 of 1,567 eligible TRAIN labels) and set gas to 0.28 when its posterior exceeded 0.8. Its exact ZIP lost completion 10/12 versus selected ZIP 11/12 on confirmation 5212–5215. Four fixed hand-designed corrections did not produce a fresh completion-safe candidate: speed saturation lost to the original student on fresh TUNE 5216–5219; far-object suppression and burst cooldown each traded one repaired consumed failure for one lost consumed finish; a finish-view signal fired early in normal runs. A fixed half-strength gas blend repaired one consumed failure but finished 10/12 versus selected 11/12 on fresh TUNE 5220–5223. Those confirmation and TUNE identities remain consumed and may only be used for diagnosis.

The next batch will test four independent mechanisms, at most two candidates per direction and eight total:

1. **Learn teacher coasting as a separate action class.** On successful TRAIN-only pixel-control trajectories, ask the geometry teacher in shadow mode for boost, hold and coast suggestions. Train a pixel-only model to reproduce all three actions, including caution before curves. The teacher action never drives data collection and simulator geometry never enters inference. Falsify if the coast class lacks TRAIN support, is not expressed by the student, or costs valid completion.
2. **Visual time-to-contact forecast.** Use recent pixels to estimate whether a visible obstacle is approaching quickly and predict a short risk horizon. Reduce gas only before projected contact, with no simulator obstacle position in runtime. Falsify if the warning is late or triggers broadly on normal finishes.
3. **Calibrated dual-policy arbitration.** Compare the fast student and stable pixel driver in TRAIN, then learn a pixel-only risk score that gives the stable driver authority on uncertain observations. Falsify if it merely memorizes track/seed identities or lacks independent completion improvement.
4. **Continuous target-speed imitation.** Regress a TRAIN-only geometry teacher's desired speed or gas delta from rendered HUD, road bend and temporal motion rather than using a binary accelerate label. Falsify if continuous predictions collapse to uniform boost or fail to activate safe coasting.

For direction 1, first register a TRAIN-only shadow-label collection and within-TRAIN validation plan. The decisive activation endpoint is nontrivial coast labels on successful pixel-control runs, finite validation loss, and pixel-policy coasting in a registered TRAIN driving diagnostic without losing valid completion. Only after activation may it use untouched TUNE identities selected in a separate plan. Other directions remain alternatives, not silent fallbacks. Every cycle fixes source hashes, control, checkpoint, split/cell identities, denominator, complete condition, falsifier and resource limit before execution. Compare valid completion first and lap time only after a tie; carry every failure into the cumulative evidence. No official upload, model confirmation or submission is included.
