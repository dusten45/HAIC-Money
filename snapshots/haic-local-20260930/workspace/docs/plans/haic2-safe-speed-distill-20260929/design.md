# Safe-trajectory pixel speed distillation: TRAIN gate

The geometry-coast teacher shortened some TRAIN laps but its own pedal actions repeatedly lost valid finishes. This plan does not execute teacher actions. On tracks 1–3 × previously unused TRAIN seeds 5012–5015, drive the selected stable-risk pixel control and query the frozen geometry teacher in shadow mode at each visited state. Keep feature/label pairs only from control episodes that validly finish, and only from decisions where the control is not braking, steering strongly or seeing an obstacle. The label is whether the geometry teacher would raise gas above the control gas. This avoids learning from the teacher's failed trajectory, though a counterfactual acceleration remains unproven safe.

Train a small pixel-derived feature model on nine TRAIN cells; reserve the final seed per track as within-TRAIN diagnostic validation. Inputs are rendered road and speed features plus the stable pixel driver's own action. No simulator state, map coordinate, seed or geometry enters student inference. The student may only raise gas to 0.28 on visually clear, low-steer decisions when its fixed posterior exceeds 0.8; inherited steering/braking and obstacle protection remain authoritative. Save a checkpoint and a report; do not save raw observations or training labels.

The registered TRAIN gate requires at least 100 positive labels in finished control trajectories, finite training/validation losses, and at least 50 predicted gas changes on within-TRAIN validation observations. This establishes mechanism activation only, not driving improvement. A separate exact-hash plan on disjoint TUNE cells must establish completion at least equal to the selected control, zero candidate-only nonfinish or invalid actions, and a faster paired median lap before held-out work. Official submission, model confirmation and site upload are excluded.
