# TRAIN-only geometry speed teacher feasibility

Earlier full-state teachers V1–V5 did not produce a fast, reliably finished trajectory on TRAIN 1:43 and 2:102. Their full steering control diverged from the road after high acceleration. This probe keeps the frozen stable-risk pixel driver's steering and its obstacle safety response. A TRAIN-only teacher reads the environment's track geometry and actual speed to set an anticipatory longitudinal speed target. No simulator state or map enters a submission-time agent.

The teacher computes curvature over nearby track segments, propagates a braking-distance speed ceiling backward from upcoming bends, and raises gas only while below that ceiling and the inherited actor is not braking or strongly steering. It can suppress gas and brake before a bend. This is a new information and control mechanism, not a sweep of the previous pixel brake thresholds. Keep the teacher source and checkpoint fixed for this probe.

Use already-consumed TRAIN cells 1:43 and 2:102, two arms (stable-risk control and geometry-speed teacher), 2,000 decisions each, 600-second Linux CPU limit. Record valid finishes, lap times, progress, collisions, damage, gas/brake intervention counts, speeds, action latency and invalid actions. The teacher must finish both cells and lower median finished lap time by at least 3% relative to the paired control before any demonstration collection or distillation is considered. A teacher-only result is not a submission-candidate completion rate. If this small feasibility gate fails, do not spend a fresh tune cell or train a pixel student from the failed teacher. No official site action is included.
