## Four independent directions

1. **Privileged recovery teacher, then pixel distillation.** At a road-visibility failure in TRAIN, a teacher reads simulator track and car pose to target a physically reachable nearby road segment while retaining the frozen visual mode-switch outside recovery. This tests whether recovery is controllable at all from that state before asking a pixel student to imitate it. Falsifier: the teacher cannot preserve valid finishes or shortens visibility loss while causing a later invalid finish. First diagnostic: register exact consumed TRAIN cells and intervention events, compare teacher/control from a shared action prefix, and keep teacher outcomes out of submission-candidate completion rates.
2. **Visual motion odometry.** Infer lateral drift and heading from the four-frame pixel stack while the road is still visible; propagate that state briefly through road occlusion, then steer according to estimated motion rather than a fixed side or fixed magnitude. Falsifier: estimated drift/heading diverges on held-back whole TRAIN cells, or runtime actions never differ before road loss. It needs an observation-only TRAIN feasibility gate before driving.
3. **Interventional action-value labels.** On newly registered TRAIN cells, replay exact common prefixes and apply alternative recovery actions at sampled visibility-loss events. Label the actual qualified finish and time differences. Train a pixel-only gate only if enough independent events and held-back-cell accuracy exist; passive prediction error is insufficient. Falsifier: intervention outcomes cannot be predicted out of cell or the gate collapses to one action. Do not train on the consumed TUNE failures.
4. **Pixel finish-crossing localization.** Detect the physical finish approach from an image sequence and maintain a crossing trajectory only after an independent pixel-only qualification cue. This targets late invalid laps separately from mid-lap road loss. Falsifier: early false triggers in successful ordinary laps or no timely triggers before invalid crossings. Prior single-view finish detectors had false positives, so a fresh temporal observability gate is required before driving.

At most one candidate per direction enters the first batch. Each executable cycle registers the exact source snapshot, plan hash, split, map/seed identities, control, denominator, outcome and resource limit before its standing-authority design/execution events. Compare qualified completion before median finished lap on new matched cells. TUNE, held-out, confirmation and blind stay disjoint. No official upload, model confirmation or submission is authorized.

The next smallest experiment is direction 1's TRAIN-only oracle feasibility gate. If the geometry teacher fails to recover from a shared prefix without losing finishes, reject that teacher and advance to direction 2; do not tune the failed road-memory blend on consumed TUNE.
