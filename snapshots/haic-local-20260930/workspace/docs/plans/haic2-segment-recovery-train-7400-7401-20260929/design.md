# Four pixel recovery mechanisms on fresh TRAIN cells

The prior offline student's natural `switch_count` mixed two separate visibility-loss segments. Five natural segments had no within-segment teacher direction switch, but the earlier six-step ±0.7 hard rule lost a valid control finish. This batch compares four **independent mechanisms** that change different signals or actuators around the frozen bend-rebound driver, rather than sweeping that hard rule's gain.

Common matched set: tracks 1–3 × fresh TRAIN seeds 7400–7401, six cells per arm. Arms are exact bend-rebound control and four candidate mechanisms, 30 episodes total, 650 decisions/episode. Control ZIP SHA-256 `8f7e87c29e92caf49db0070842dbc30f21a20ff98a3f48bf17d9599490559e49`. All actions use pixels and reset-local memory only. No geometry, map/seed identity or official score enters agent actions. The simulator result measures valid finish and lap time. This is a TRAIN screen, not a final selected ZIP.

1. **Segment motion guard.** On first frame with fewer than three visible road-center rows, use the latest valid row-54 motion of at least one pixel from the four-frame stack. Retain its sign until the road has at least three rows again; while lost, make steering at least 0.18 toward that sign. No gas/brake change. The segment reset corrects the episode-wide memory error.
2. **Visibility brake.** During fewer than three visible rows and pixel-estimated speed above 35, set gas zero and brake at least 0.08; leave steering inherited. Reduced stopping distance may preserve a finish through an uncertain road boundary.
3. **Reacquisition coast.** After road visibility returns to at least three rows, set gas zero for four decisions while speed exceeds 40; leave steering inherited. This aims to prevent an immediate post-recovery surge.
4. **Offcenter asphalt seek.** If road-center detection fails, inspect the longest contiguous asphalt segment of at least eight pixels on row 54. When its center is at least six pixels off image center, steer at least 0.20 toward it; leave pedals inherited. This uses lateral road evidence outside the central search window.

Activation requires at least three changed actions per candidate and zero invalid actions. For each candidate, reject any candidate-only lost control finish regardless of lap time. Among arms with matched completion counts and no candidate-only loss, compare median finished lap time, mean incomplete progress, P90 finished lap time, collisions, damage, then p95 act latency. At least one activated candidate must beat control on that order to advance to a separately registered fresh TUNE comparison. If none does, `PIVOT` or `REVISE` based on whether the mechanism activated. A six-cell observation alone does not establish generalization. Source stays frozen through gate review. Local Windows CPU resource cap: 1,200 seconds, two cores, 2 GiB. No external action.
