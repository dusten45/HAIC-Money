# TRAIN-only temporal pixel risk feasibility from the confirmed mode switch

This is direction 1 of the data-driven four-direction batch. Frozen local control is the exact visual mode-switch ZIP SHA-256 `b0dc7a24e6ef994cee6e663ec6605f5aa088dbb9b3ce4510f90ccd16dc9d7fc4`, which completed 12/12 extracted-ZIP confirmation cells with median 24.07 s. This local baseline is not an official score or current-site compliance claim. Earlier hand-designed mode-speed changes lost valid TUNE completions, so this cycle asks whether short-horizon risk is predictably visible before changing any driving actions.

On previously unused TRAIN tracks 1-3 x seeds 5044-5047, run frozen mode-switch and its unchanged fast-only actor on the same twelve cells, 24 episodes. Only fast-only trajectories supply risk labels; mode-switch provides paired local control behavior. The training and validation split is 5044-5046 versus 5047 within TRAIN for each track. At each fast-only state with at least five visible road rows, no pixel obstacle, rendered speed at least 25 and no safe-policy brake, record 24 features: 21 rendered-pixel/fast-action features plus the three differences between fast and safe pixel actions. Label a state risky when collision or rendered road visibility below three rows at speed at least 30 occurs in the next twelve decisions of that same fast-only trajectory. Labels use only local TRAIN future outcomes and do not enter runtime. No simulator geometry, coordinates, progress, track or seed identity enters features.

Train one fixed 32-16 ReLU classifier, Adam 0.002 for 40 epochs with bounded positive weighting. Freeze its decision threshold at the 80th percentile of negative TRAIN scores, before validation. Activation gate: at least 40 positive training and 10 positive validation states; validation AUC at least 0.70, recall at least 0.50 and false-positive rate at most 0.25 at that frozen threshold; safe tensor-only checkpoint round trip; zero invalid actions and unchanged frozen control behavior. If the gate fails, reject without model/threshold sweep on these cells. If it passes, register a separate fresh TRAIN driving comparison in which the risk estimate changes mode choice; this run does not drive a learned student and contributes no submission-candidate completion rate. Each episode max 2,000 decisions, Linux CPU run timeout 1,800 seconds. No TUNE, held-out, confirmation, blind or external action.
