## Registered direction within the four-direction data-driven batch

This develops direction 2 of [the four-direction data-driven batch](haic2-mode-switch-data-driven-batch-20260929.md), alongside its independent temporal risk, policy distillation and episode routing directions. It is a feasibility study before candidate driving, not a parameter sweep. Causal path: grayscale road motion and chosen action → predicted future road center/visibility → choose safe or fast action when predicted visible-road margin is adequate → qualified finish with shorter lap. The submitted runtime may use only pixel-derived current/recent road and HUD features, selected actions and private history. Simulator speed/position/collision are diagnostic labels only and cannot enter runtime.

Use a frozen TRAIN-only episode partition, holding back complete map/seed cells rather than random decisions from the same run. Before fitting, preregister the exact TRAIN cells, target horizon, model class, loss, action inputs, no-action baseline, persistence baseline, minimum improvement, compute budget, and failure conditions in a v2 plan. A useful activation gate must show out-of-TRAIN-cell error improvement over persistence **and** an action-ablation test proving that supplied action affects prediction on held-back cells; a mode chooser also has to change real actions. If action sensitivity cannot be identified from natural policy rollouts, collect explicitly interventional TRAIN trajectories on new seeds under a registered profile rather than claim a counterfactual planner. Keep TUNE, held-out, confirmation and private official tracks unopened for this model-selection task. No competition-site action.
