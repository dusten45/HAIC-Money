# High-speed recovery pivot — design revision 1

## Intent and evidence

The user wants a reliable local finisher that approaches public top-three lap times, accepts some grass driving, and never uploads or confirms a model at the competition site. Public historical ghost video shows only screen-space paths, not leader actions. The selected full-road-guard ZIP remains the local control. The exact speed-coupled ZIP finished 11/12 against the control's 12/12 on untouched held-out 2004–2007 despite a 1.01-second faster median among its finished laps. Its candidate-only track 3/2006 retirement was off-track at 77.86% progress with two collisions. This held-out cell is evidence of failure, not tuning input.

Read-only inspection of already consumed tune trace `runs/haic-research-v2/speed-coupled-preview-road-excursion-trace-20260929/events.jsonl` found a separate track 1/2002 failure mode: the speed candidate had no detected road centers and all four wheels off asphalt for decisions 182–230. Progress remained 61.6% while simulator speed rose from 37.8 to 46.9; mean gas was 0.044, brake 0. The last partial road before loss lay to the left; the steering reversed from about −0.42 at decision 176 to +0.14 by decision 181. It later rejoined and finished, so this is a mechanism hypothesis, not proof of the cause of track 3/2006.

## Four independent directions

Every candidate uses only grayscale frames and its own action history at inference; simulator progress, wheel contacts, map coordinates, and obstacle ground truth are evaluation-only. The exact selected control ZIP is included in matched comparisons. Existing speed-coupled ZIP behavior is a reference arm, not promoted by this design.

1. **Road-loss memory and deceleration.** Preserve the last visible near/far road direction. When road centers disappear, stop extra gas, apply bounded braking at speed, and steer toward the last coherent road direction for a short horizon. This targets the measured no-road accelerating interval. Activation evidence: actions differ specifically after road visibility loss and the road becomes visible again before an extended negative-reward streak. Falsifier: no action change, invalid action, or lower finish count than control.
2. **Path curvature and exit alignment.** Fit near/middle/far road centers across consecutive frames, aim at a stable point toward the bend exit, and damp steering reversal until the near center confirms the exit. This changes the target line rather than only reducing gas. Activation evidence: shifted steering before the bend and fewer sign reversals in a visible U/V turn. Falsifier: no measured change or lower finish count.
3. **Obstacle-side passage.** Use the visible bright-object location relative to both road boundaries to choose a pass side while there is enough width, then maintain that side until the object leaves the near field. This changes the route around hazards rather than only applying earlier brake. Activation evidence: stable same-side steering before a visible obstacle and no added collisions in TRAIN. Falsifier: no route change, invalid action, or lower finish count.
4. **High-speed preview with speed budget.** Estimate speed from pixels and use curvature and recent lateral road motion together to set a speed target before the turn; release gas on stable straights and reduce it only ahead of bends. This explicitly couples early steering and speed instead of a fixed turn threshold or unconditional throttle increase. Activation evidence: higher gas on straight intervals, lower gas before a visible bend, and a different speed trajectory. Falsifier: no state/action change or lower finish count.

The directions are distinct by trigger and state change: absent-road recovery, visible-road path target, visible-obstacle route choice, and speed target. They are not four threshold settings of one controller.

## Registered evaluation path

First, a frozen-source TRAIN activation screen on already designated train cells 1/43 and 2/102 with the selected control and at most one candidate per direction; at most 10 episodes and 2,000 decisions each. TRAIN outcomes are diagnostics only. Freeze the four activated candidates before opening new tune tracks 1–3 × seeds 2012–2015, twelve matched cells per arm, with 2016–2019 reserved for held-out and 2020–2023 for unchanged-package confirmation. No reserved held-out or confirmation cell is opened for tuning. Preflight project v2 manifests for identity collisions and record exact map, split, denominator, package/source hashes, plan hash, permission and resource limits before the execution gate.

At design time, a read-only scan of 157 v2 `run_manifest.json` files under the current and frozen research roots found no `seed_ids` overlap with track/seed pairs 1–3 × 2012–2023. Repeat this check immediately before registration because other local work may consume cells meanwhile.

Completion is the qualified finish-line crossing, not progress 1.0 or reward. Compare eligible candidates on the same cells by completion count first; on a tie use median finished lap, mean incomplete progress, P90 finished lap, collisions, damage, then p95 act latency. Record action changes, road visibility, corner-entry/exit arrival, obstacle-side choice, gas/brake, invalid actions, damage, and evaluator-only road contact. Grass contact is a diagnostic and not an automatic veto. One candidate-only nonfinish is reported even if another cell balances the total. Run Linux Python 3.11 CPU, 2 CPUs, 2 GiB, at most 2,000 decisions per episode and 1,800 seconds per stage. The local CLI must record design, implementation, execution approvals against each immutable plan hash, plus original events and three-gate integration. No site upload, official submission, or model confirmation.
