# Visual obstacle lockout for a TRAIN-only geometry speed teacher

The fixed geometry-coast teacher gained 5.0% median lap time on ten jointly completed TRAIN cells but failed one cell that the stable pixel control completed. Near that failure, the teacher hit the same obstacle three times and then remained off track. The causal role of earlier gas is uncertain. The revised mechanism returns the pixel driver's entire action whenever an obstacle is visible in the rendered pixels and for the next 20 decisions. This preserves the driver's steering, gas and brake through the approach and exit instead of letting the geometry teacher reaccelerate during the hazard. On clear road, the teacher retains its frozen geometry-speed acceleration and coasting.

The control is the unchanged stable-risk pixel driver and selected checkpoint. The candidate is training-only and may read geometry and speed for teacher actions; obstacle lockout uses only rendered pixels. Compare both arms on tracks 1–3 × new TRAIN seeds 5008–5011, 12 cells each, 2,000 decisions per episode, 1,800 seconds maximum. The gate is completion first: candidate must finish at least as many cells, have no candidate-only nonfinish, zero invalid actions, and improve median lap by at least 3% on jointly completed paired cells. Record guard decisions, gas/coast interventions, all episode traces, collisions, damage, latency and duration. This is a new plan/hash; failed 5004–5007 remain consumed. No student training or external action follows automatically from this evaluation.
