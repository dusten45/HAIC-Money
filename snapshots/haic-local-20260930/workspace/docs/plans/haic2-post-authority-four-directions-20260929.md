# Four completion-first directions after bounded-authority held-out failure

The fixed bounded-authority pixel student improved fresh TRAIN completion 12/12 versus selected 11/12 and fresh TUNE 11/12 versus selected 8/12, but failed untouched held-out 11/12 versus selected 12/12. The candidate-only 2:5231 off-track episode reached progress 1.0 without a valid lap. Post-hoc simulator trace shows a route divergence around decisions 260-300, when candidate speed and gas exceeded selected control ahead of a bend, followed by a progress plateau near 0.743 and a long missed-finish loop. The simulator coordinates and progress are diagnosis only and may never become inference inputs. The held-out identities are consumed and will not be used for tuning or fresh score. No exact ZIP or external action occurred.

The next batch has four independent mechanisms and at most one candidate per direction initially, four candidates total. Source, checkpoint, split identities, denominator, completion endpoint, falsifier and resource limits will be fixed in separate v2 plan hashes before execution. Consumed cells may support observation-only diagnosis; driving comparisons use newly assigned TRAIN, then disjoint TUNE, held-out and exact-ZIP confirmation. Completion ranks before lap time. A single repaired cell is a diagnostic, not a generalization claim.

## Direction 1: bend-phase gas veto

Requirement: learned gas must not cause the car to enter a bend faster than the visible road permits. Observable at inference: current and prior pixel road centers, their far-to-near bend and approach rate, HUD speed, and inherited pixel action. Permitted change: suppress only the learned extra gas during an approaching high-curvature phase, leaving inherited steering and braking. Expected endpoint: visible pre-bend gas suppression, no lost valid completions, and improved paired time only if completion ties. Control: frozen bounded-authority student and selected pixel driver on the same new cells. Falsifier: the signal appears only after departure, suppresses ordinary straight driving, or reduces completion. Smallest experiment: consumed-cell observation-only lead-time check, followed by a frozen 12-cell fresh TRAIN comparison if observable. Resource/risk: one registered local diagnostic at a time, no reserved split data in learning.

## Direction 2: visual route-health recovery

Requirement: recover valid laps when the pixel road appears to drift or disappear despite continued motion. Observable at inference: temporal road-center continuity, visible road count, HUD motion and recent commanded steering; no simulator progress or position. Permitted change: transiently hand full action authority to the stable pixel driver when a persistent visual route-health state activates, then return only after stable visual reacquisition. Expected endpoint: timely activation before departure, preserved normal finishes, and higher valid completion. Control: the same frozen bounded-authority student without health routing. Falsifier: alarm occurs only after road loss, fires broadly on ordinary turns, or makes a previously valid finish fail. Smallest experiment: observation-only replay on consumed failures and successes, then fresh TRAIN matched driving. Resource/risk: finite bounded memory, pixel-only runtime, no map identity.

## Direction 3: continuous teacher speed target

Requirement: represent caution and acceleration as a speed target rather than a mostly positive boost class. Observable at inference: rendered HUD speed, pixel road bend and temporal movement. TRAIN-only shadow teacher may use geometry to label desired speed, but does not drive data collection and never enters runtime. Permitted change: a pixel-only regressor controls gas increment and coast based on predicted target speed; inherited steering and braking remain. Expected endpoint: noncollapsed target predictions, both positive and negative gas changes, and completion-safe lap improvement. Control: selected stable pixel driver and prior bounded-authority student on registered new TRAIN cells. Falsifier: predictions collapse to uniform boost, fail calibration outside TRAIN, or reduce valid completion. Smallest experiment: successful-control TRAIN label collection and within-TRAIN validation, then a separately registered fresh driving comparison. Resource/risk: frozen tensor-only checkpoint and no held-out labels.

## Direction 4: early pixel episode routing

Requirement: choose a reliable policy for a whole episode when early visual conditions predict a completion tradeoff. Observable at inference: the first few rendered road/HUD frames and both pixel policies' local actions, with no map or seed identity. Permitted change: select either frozen stable pixel control or frozen bounded-authority student once near episode start; no mid-episode switching. Expected endpoint: out-of-TRAIN predictions that reduce candidate-only failures without discarding all speed gains. Control: both fixed policies and an always-selected baseline on matched new cells. Falsifier: early pixels do not predict paired outcomes, route choices collapse to one arm, or fresh completion does not improve. Smallest experiment: TRAIN-only paired outcome labels, held-back TRAIN validation, then fresh TUNE if activation passes. Resource/risk: no explicit map IDs or simulator state in runtime and no tuning on consumed held-out cells.

No direction is promoted based on code presence or consumed-cell repair. The next concrete step is an observation-only bend/route-health diagnostic on consumed failures, with a registered plan and immutable source copy. Official upload, model confirmation and submission remain outside scope.
