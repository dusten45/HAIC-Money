# Pixel recovery student observability before fitting

Read-only audit of the frozen, consumed TRAIN 5160–5161 activation data (six map/seed cells, 12 induced pulse episodes, 248 teacher decisions). These cells are not held-out student validation and this observation does not change their registered `ADVANCE` decision.

At the first teacher intervention in each induced episode, the sign of the near-road center movement between the two preceding frames agreed with the teacher steering sign in **12/12 episodes**. This is a small, pulse-induced observation, not an estimated generalization rate. In seven of the twelve episodes the teacher steering sign later changed. At all seven sign changes the newest frame had no detected near-road center; one had zero road-center rows in all four frames. Therefore a pixel student that simply memorizes its first correction direction cannot be assumed to imitate the teacher through a long visibility loss.

The separately registered TRAIN 5170–5177 collection will provide whole-cell fitting and validation data, including unpulsed episodes. Before any student driving, report first-intervention direction accuracy and later sign-change accuracy separately on held-back whole TRAIN cells. Compare against a fixed first-direction hold rule, inherited driver steer, and the prior last-seen-road-side rule. If later sign changes are not observable from pixels and action history, limit the student's intervention to a short bounded recovery segment or reject the student; do not promote teacher-only completion as a candidate score. The exact bend-rebound ZIP remains the local driving control.

## Later TRAIN observations

The completed 5170–5177 collection yielded 48 labeled episodes. The most recent valid near-road-center movement matched the first teacher-steer sign in **48/48** episodes, including two unpulsed natural episodes. A separately registered fresh natural-only block on 5292–5299 yielded four more natural episodes; the same preexisting sign rule matched **4/4** first labels. These small natural counts support an initial direction signal but do not establish that a fixed-duration steering correction completes a lap: the six-decision action rule repaired 1:5176 and lost valid finish 3:5176. The student feasibility study must evaluate complete recovery sequences and eventual driving, not first-label sign alone.
