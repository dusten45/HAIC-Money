# Fixed pulse activation for balanced recovery labels

This is a diagnostic continuation of [the balanced recovery design](../../../docs/plans/haic2-balanced-recovery-collection-design-20260929.md). The Docker-interrupted passive collection is preserved and excluded from fitting. On each fresh TRAIN track 1-3 × seed 5140-5141 cell, run the exact bend-rebound ZIP controller as unpulsed control, left-pulse teacher, and right-pulse teacher. The two pulse arms inject exactly eight decisions of steer -0.7/+0.7 after decision 80 when seven pixel-road rows are visible, the near road center is within six pixels of image center and no pixel obstacle is detected. Gas and brake remain inherited. The privileged centerline teacher then acts only below three visible road rows. Save real unmirrored pixel stacks and teacher labels, and hash any NPZ file. Simulator geometry is used only to create TRAIN labels, never pulse timing or runtime student inputs.

Registered denominator: six independent map/seed cells and 18 episodes (three arms/cell). This is teacher-only activation evidence, not candidate completion scoring. The gate requires 18 episodes attempted, valid file hashes, finite samples/actions/labels and zero invalid actions. Mechanism activation requires full eight-step pulses in at least four distinct cells for each pulse arm, positive teacher steering in at least three distinct left-pulse cells, and negative steering in at least three distinct right-pulse cells. Failure revises state generation on a new TRAIN block, without sweeping pulse parameters on these cells. Success permits a separate large TRAIN collection, then whole-cell student fit/validation. No TUNE, held-out, confirmation, blind, ZIP promotion or external action.

Resource cap: Linux CPU, two cores, 2 GiB, 1,800 seconds, 2,000 decisions per episode. Exact SHA-pinned bend-rebound ZIP is the control and inherited driver.
